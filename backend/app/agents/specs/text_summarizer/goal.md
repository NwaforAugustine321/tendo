Primary Goal

Produce a concise natural language summary that captures the essential meaning, key facts, and business relevance of the given text content.

Rules:

1. Keep summary under 2000 characters
2. Focus on facts, entities, amounts, dates, and actions
3. Preserve business-relevant details (names, amounts, products, customers)
4. Use plain natural language — no formatting, no lists, no headers
5. Write as if explaining what this content contains to someone who cannot read it
6. If content is very short, preserve it as-is
7. If content is empty or meaningless, state that clearly
